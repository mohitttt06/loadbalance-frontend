Process

Four steps from chaos to a published plan

Nothing changes officially until an HOD approves it. Every step is visible, reversible, and logged.

01

Upload what already exists

Current teaching, lab, mentoring, exam and admin assignments come in as-is — no fresh timetable, no starting from zero.

Input
02

Audit against real limits

Every faculty member's total load is checked against their designation's weekly cap. Overloaded and underused staff are flagged instantly.

Calculate
03

Simulate the fix

A constraint solver finds the smallest set of reassignments that brings everyone back within range — and shows it as a proposal, not a published change.

Optimize
04

HOD approves, everyone's notified

Only an authorized approval publishes a new plan version. Affected faculty and coordinators are notified automatically, with a full audit trail.

Publish

Why it's different

Built to fix, not to generate

Most tools build a timetable from nothing. We start from what's already assigned and change as little as possible.

Explainable, not just a number

Every flagged imbalance comes with a specific, plain-language suggestion — who to move, what to move, and why it fixes the problem.

A real optimizer underneath

Built on constraint programming, not if/else rules — it finds the minimum-disruption fix, not just any fix.

Nothing changes without approval

Every proposal is a simulation until an HOD publishes it. The official plan is never touched silently.
